Objective Move every residential security deposit into bonded custodial escrow, replace unilateral deposit deductions with independent adjudication, and back verified tenant hardship with a three-source rent assurance pool — with zero subrogation debt.

Core mechanics at a glance

Title I · Deposit Escrow · I-1

Landlords never hold deposits; neutral adjudicators decide claims.

Deposits sit in bonded custodial escrow for the tenancy. Tenants earn statutory 1% interest plus clean-year surplus dividends; pooled net float (~3%, ~$23.4M/yr at scale) funds administration and independent adjudication.

Title II · Rent Assurance · II-3

Verified hardship rent is paid from a three-source pool — never clawed back.

During verified job loss or medical crisis, the pool pays covered rent directly to the housing provider. Funded by ~3% deposit float (~$3.25/mo), monthly M1PR tax-credit advance (~$6/mo), and matched landlord share (~$6/mo).

Fairness & Anti-Capture · I-4

Symmetric fraud penalties, public claims registry, and a 5-year sunset.

Paid claims end the matter (no subrogation). Fabricated landlord claims and false fraud referrals carry symmetric penalties; tenant abuse is bounded by verified triggers, per-lease caps, and renewal pricing.

Base funding braid at $1,300/mo rent: $3.25 deposit float + $6.04 M1PR monthly advance + $6.04 landlord match = $15.33/mo (~1.18% of rent). Test any rent or building size →
