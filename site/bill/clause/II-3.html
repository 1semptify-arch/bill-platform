The draft / II-3

II-3 — Three-source funding braid & mutual dividends modeled

Participation is mandatory statewide upon rollout and funded by three braided legs (~$15.33/unit/month base case at $1,300 rent, ~1.18% of rent): (a) Escrowed deposit net float (~$3.25/unit/mo); (b) Renter-side share (~$6/unit/mo) paid as a monthly advance from the Minnesota Renter's Credit (M1PR) reconciled on the annual return via CRP e-file rails (federal APTC / Form 8962 model; over-cap renters pay ~$6/mo directly, disclosed); and (c) Landlord matched share (~$6/unit/mo) priced on a community-rated base plus bounded experience band, earning a no-claim dividend in surplus years.

In plain language: Roughly $15.33/month per unit comes from three small shares: ~$3.25 from deposit interest float, ~$6 advanced from the existing MN Renter's Credit (or paid directly by high-income renters), and ~$6 matched by the landlord (who gets a rebate dividend in clean years).

  • Renter: For M1PR-eligible renters (up to $77,570 income), your ~$6/mo share comes from your existing credit — $0 new out-of-pocket.
  • Housing provider: ~$6/mo (~$72/yr) replaces $5,600–$7,400 eviction losses, with mutual rebates for clean buildings.
  • Lawmaker & staff: Rides existing DOR M1PR ($2,720 max) + mandatory CRP e-file rails; split ratio awaits actuarial sign-off.

Standing on

  • $15.33/mo (1.18%) — Base-case three-source pooled cost per unit at $1,300 monthly rent (~$106M claims vs. ~$110M net funding). estimate (Turnbuchalla Math Audit 2026-10-23 (whitepaper L99))
  • $21.23/mo (1.63%) — Fraud/moral-hazard stress scenario (+30% claims shock above base case). estimate (Turnbuchalla Stress Model (whitepaper L100))
  • $30.00/mo (2.31%) — Severe recession shock scenario (6% statewide lease default rate); ~$4M/yr reserve build in normal years. estimate (Turnbuchalla Stress Model (whitepaper L101–L104))
  • ~$23.4M/yr (3% net) — Net annual custodial deposit float on ~$780M escrowed statewide deposits (4% Treasury yield minus 1% tenant floor). estimate (Turnbuchalla Float Restatement 2026-10-23 (whitepaper L114))
  • $2,720 max / $77,570 cap — Minnesota Renter's Credit (M1PR) maximum annual credit and household income eligibility cap. verified (Minnesota Department of Revenue — TY2025 M1PR Schedule)
