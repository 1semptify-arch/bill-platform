Three-source funding braid & stress test

Test any Minnesota monthly rent, portfolio unit count, and M1PR eligibility against the 2026-10-23 math audit (Clause II-3). Figures marked estimate carry transparent derivations awaiting formal actuarial review.

Leg 1 · Escrowed Deposit Float (3% net)

$3.25/unit/mo

4% Treasury yield on 1-month escrowed deposit minus the tenant's statutory 1% interest floor ($13.00/yr kept by renter).

Leg 2 · Renter Share (M1PR Advance)

$6.04/unit/mo

$0.00 / mo new out-of-pocket (covered via monthly M1PR tax-credit advance)

Leg 3 · Landlord Matched Share

$6.04/unit/mo

Community-rated base + bounded experience band; clean years earn a mutual no-claim dividend.

Combined Assurance Pool (~1.18% of rent)

$15.33/unit/mo

Annual landlord match across 1 unit(s): $72.48/yr vs. $5,600–$7,400 cost of a single eviction (89.7 unit-years of match = 1 avoided eviction).

Actuarial stress scenarios (per unit / month)

Base case (~1.18% of rent · ~$106M statewide claims) $15.33 / mo
Fraud / moral-hazard stress (+30% claims shock · ~1.63% of rent) $21.23 / mo
Recession shock (6% statewide lease default · ~2.31% of rent) $30.00 / mo
