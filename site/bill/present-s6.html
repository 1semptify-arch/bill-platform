Slide 6 of 8 · Fiscal Discipline & Rollout Sequence

Stress-Tested Numbers & MHFA Pilot Path

Three modeled loss regimes (at $1,300 rent)

Base case (1.18%): $15.33 / unit / mo (~$106M claims)

+30% Fraud / hazard shock (1.63%): $21.23 / unit / mo

6% Recession default shock (2.31%): $30.00 / unit / mo

Normal years build ~$4M/yr in dedicated reserves.

Clause II-6 & IV-4 · Prove it first, sunset if it misses

Step 1: MHFA operates Title II as a pilot on LIHTC subsidized properties using existing compliance rails.

Step 2: Audited pilot loss data forms the official fiscal note for statewide rollout.

Step 3: Mandatory Year-3 committee audit + hard 5-year statutory sunset.
