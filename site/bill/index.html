Introduction — what this is

This platform presents a real, working bill — the Minnesota Rent Assurance & Deposit Protection Act — the way a bill should be shown to the public: the full statutory text in 23 clauses, the plain English beside it, the math behind it, and the record of every mark, request, and change it has ever taken.

It is living, not a PDF. The draft updates as it is written, every change lands on the Editions ledger, and every figure is marked verified or estimate. Nothing here is filed law — but nothing here is hidden either.

How it works

  1. Read it your way. The draft at /bill carries statute text and plain language side by side, with audience lenses for renters, housing providers, and lawmakers. Three screen modes — Desktop, TV / Present, and Mobile / Tablet — fit whatever screen you're on.
  2. Mark the draft. Any clause takes a mark — a question, a comment, or a proposed edit with your own wording — through the Inkwell. Highlight text on a clause and the toolbar offers to quote it directly.
  3. Marks get read in by a person. Nothing posts automatically. Marks land in a dark intake queue; a steward reads each in (it gets its public M-number), holds it, or crisis-routes it — someone in housing trouble is pointed at help, not published.
  4. The ledger answers in the open. Read-in marks live on the Margin — numbered, dated, attached to their clause, answered by the drafting table, and replyable by reference (re: M-0001). 4 on the record so far.
  5. Requests get worked, not lost. Briefings, amendments, positions, and asks go through the Requests desk: new → reviewing → scheduled → incorporated. 2 active.
  6. The math is checkable. The funding braid calculator runs the live three-source model — deposit float, M1PR advance, landlord match — against any rent, portfolio size, and stress scenario.
