M-0004 on II-6 · Nonprofit Housing Developer

Starting Title II as an MHFA pilot on LIHTC properties is the right sequence — those properties already submit standardized compliance reporting, so loss-ratio data will be clean for committee fiscal staff.

M-0003 on I-2 · Housing Policy Researcher

In UK custodial schemes, clear timelines keep units turning over without holding up either party. Adding an explicit 14-day adjudication clock in I-2 would help both small landlords and moving renters.

Answer: Strong operational point — logged for Title I statutory text integration alongside Minn. Stat. § 504B.178's existing 21-day baseline.

M-0002 on I-4 · Small Duplex Owner (4 units, Duluth)

If there is zero subrogation, what stops a bad-faith tenant from trashing a unit or skipping rent intentionally and walking away clean?

Answer: Three statutory controls in Clauses I-7 and II-5: (1) intentional/criminal destruction is excluded from hardship coverage; (2) hardship triggers require UI-grade verification (involuntary job loss or documented medical crisis); and (3) paid-claim history prices future renewal tiers and caps per-lease payouts.
