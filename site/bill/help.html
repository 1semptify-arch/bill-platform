Help lines

If the bill questions brought up something heavier — you're not alone, and these are free, public help lines. Every entry is an official public channel. Nothing here is an advertisement and nobody paid to be listed.

Right now

  • Emergency — 911
  • Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988 · 24/7, free, confidential. Veterans press 1.
  • Crisis Text Line — text HOME to 741741
  • Poison Control — 1-800-222-1222

Housing

  • HOME Line — 612-728-5767 · free legal help for Minnesota renters — deposits, evictions, repairs, notices.
  • United Way 211 — dial 2-1-1 · statewide referrals for shelter, rent help, utilities, food.
  • Minnesota Food HelpLine — 1-888-711-1151 · food shelves and SNAP help.

Mental health & addiction

  • SAMHSA National Helpline — 1-800-662-4357 · 24/7 free, confidential treatment referral for substance use and mental health.
  • Alcoholics Anonymous · local meetings — aa.org
  • Narcotics Anonymous · local meetings — na.org
  • Al-Anon · for family & friends of drinkers — al-anon.org
  • Minnesota Problem Gambling Helpline — 1-800-333-4673 (1-800-333-HOPE) · free, confidential, 24/7.
  • Gamblers Anonymous · local meetings — gamblersanonymous.org
  • Debtors Anonymous · local meetings — debtorsanonymous.org

Safety & legal

  • MN Day One Crisis Line — 1-866-223-1111 · domestic violence help, statewide, 24/7.
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline — 1-800-799-7233
  • LawHelpMN.org · free legal-aid directory for Minnesota.

A note

These are public services run by their own organizations — listing a line is information, not an endorsement, and Semptify receives nothing for it. Phone numbers change; the service's official site is the authority. This page is information only, not advice.
